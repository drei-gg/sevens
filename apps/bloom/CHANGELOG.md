# Cycle & Health

## 0.1.0

- Private period, symptom, temperature, test, and sex/contact journals.
- Flexible pill packs, intervals and birth-control reminders with explicit dose history.
- Pregnancy records, appointments, cycle estimates and symptom patterns.
- Selected reports, PDF/CSV exports, encrypted backups and reviewed imports.
- Optional Apple Health flow transfer, Watch companion and discreet widgets.
- One-time family-shareable unlock; saved records remain readable, correctable and exportable.

### Android foundation

- Native journal, contact logs, plan schedules, manual dose history and basic summaries through the same Rust core.
- Keystore-backed encrypted local storage, JSON/CSV exports, encrypted portable backups and reviewed imports.
- Shared SevenKit trial and per-account unlock; approved icons supplied by the private build overlay.
- Notification delivery, Health Connect, wearable support and the remaining iOS screens are not yet connected.
