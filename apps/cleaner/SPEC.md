# Cleaner

Clean up photos, videos, selected files and contacts in one private, native app. A free seven-day trial with no card, then one purchase to unlock. Nothing renews. The first screen explains the built-in Photos, Contacts and iPhone Storage alternatives. No analytics, ads, crash reporters or app backend.

## Full product baseline

This is the product target, not a list of shipped features. Scope remains broad until implementation or acceptance work establishes a specific reason to adapt it. Treemap is deferred. Photo enhancement is excluded. Contacts cleanup is included in this app.

- **Photos:** distinguish exact resources, recompressed equivalents and similar shots; review bursts, blur/exposure candidates, screenshots, Live Photos, large images and useful local content categories. Show reasons and keeper suggestions. Filter by date, album, type, dimensions and measured size. Progressive/incremental scans must respect limited access and user decisions.
- **Videos:** exact duplicates, similar and overlapping-clip candidates; size/duration/resolution/date filters and recording collections. Playback, scrubbing, speed, audio and comparisons. Delete, compress, trim, export or keep from review.
- **Review:** grids, swipe sessions, full-frame zoom, side-by-side comparisons and useful batch actions. Select/deselect/invert, keeper rules, previewed quick cleanup, protected assets/groups/albums, keep/later and undo of pending decisions. Keep selections, scroll and checkpoints through interruptions. New scans never overwrite explicit decisions.
- **Compression:** photo/video batches, quality and resolution controls, image format conversion, Live-to-still and video trimming. Progress/cancel/retry; meaningful before/after inspection and temporary-space accounting. Separate explicit resizing from same-dimension recompression. Preserve supported capture metadata/color/orientation/alpha/audio/HDR/favorites/albums. Save and validate actual outputs before offering any original removal. Prevent repeated lossy processing.
- **Organization:** albums and favorites, searchable/sortable filing, unfiled collections where available, month/year and optional On This Day sessions. Review collections/tags remain candidates. Accessible adaptive iPhone/iPad layouts.
- **Files:** user-selected recursive folders, size/type/date filters, search, previews, exact duplicates and folder totals; permission-aware batch delete/move/export with conflicts, cancellation/resume and per-item outcomes. No access to other apps' private caches or System Data.
- **Retention:** original and multipart exports with manifests, verified destination readback before deletion, saved destinations and history. A readable JPEG is not a complete Live Photo/RAW backup.
- **Dashboard:** scan coverage, logical byte counts, unknown sizes, pending work and completed actions. Distinguish resource size, estimated reduction and observed local capacity. Explain iCloud deletion and Recently Deleted. Optional reminders/widgets remain in scope.
- **Contacts:** exact/likely candidates and match reasons, underlying account records, field-by-field merge review, incomplete entries/correction/bulk deletion, backups and restore. Respect limited access. A shared number is not identity. Prove supported fields/account preservation before destructive merging.
- **Further candidates:** email cleanup and a protected collection require separate feasibility/value evidence. No backend or destructive custody workflow is assumed.

## Preservation contract

An encode success or smaller file does not establish acceptable visual quality. Use the full original, inspect the actual saved output at useful detail, and test difficult content. Metrics can reject candidates; they cannot authorize deletion or guarantee subjective fidelity. Originals remain by default. Any future replacement is explicit and requires independent saved-output verification, supported metadata/feature preservation and a restartable mutation journal. Failure, cancellation and insufficient temporary storage must leave the source intact.

Contacts data that cannot be read must never be treated as empty. Notes need an additional Apple entitlement. A partial archive is not a lossless backup; it cannot authorize deletion. Account boundaries, linked records, labels, photos, dates, non-Gregorian birthdays, relationships and concurrent changes belong in acceptance tests.

## Current build

Implemented: native five-area navigation, SevenKit purchase/onboarding integration, accessible photo/video inventory and collections; streamed full-resource hashes and byte counts; provisional Vision similarity for nearby stills; native image zoom/video playback; batch selection, durable keep/later/removal decisions, previewed Photos deletion with revision/favorite checks; album creation/filing; selected-folder size/hash discovery; underlying contact inventory, candidate pairs, field comparison and verified partial archives; ordinary sRGB still compression candidates with metadata/detail checks and separate-copy saving. Both phones expose a 90–100% quality control with an explicit updated preview. Saving is unavailable until the selected quality has been encoded and checked. A failed retry retains the previous candidate, and every retry reads the retained original rather than recompressing the prior output. Both phones also offer Original, 2400, 1600 and 800 pixel long-edge profiles. Resizing is explicit, preserves aspect ratio, never enlarges, and requires a new preview before Save. The current output dimensions are shown beside that preview action. Detail checks compare the encoded output with its resized reference; they do not claim that discarded detail was preserved. Saving remains a separate copy and never authorizes removing the original.

Both phones now offer a selected-photo review queue of 1–10 items. The shared core validates the whole selection and retains its source/revision snapshots; each photo explicitly prepares a preview, then offers save-copy or skip. Only one original/candidate pair is prepared at a time. Native progress, cancellation during preparation, inline failure/retry, per-item results and Stop keep existing copies, selection and review decisions. Android retains the queue and current candidate across configuration changes. The queue is session-only and does not resume after app termination.

Save binds to the source snapshot that produced the preview. Native providers expose a newly created copy identifier before readback; failed readback or bookkeeping retains that receipt. Retry reads and records the same existing copy rather than creating another. Quality/size updates are disabled while a created receipt is unresolved. Source/output processed guards and history are recorded atomically together; successful outputs count only after both byte verification and that durable write. Skipping a created but unfinished receipt counts it as unverified, and the session prevents reprocessing that source/copy. Original bytes remain unchanged. Provider insertion and ledger persistence are not one crash-atomic transaction: after app termination, inspect Photos for any unrecorded copy before repeating work. Durable/background queue recovery and stronger temporary-space budgeting remain in the feature queue.

Initial compression profile: JPEG/PNG stills, 8-bit sRGB, no alpha/auxiliary data, at most 16 MP. This bounded profile is an initial implementation, not the final supported-format decision. Source replacement is not enabled. Contact destructive merges and restore-to-store are not enabled while inaccessible fields and account restoration remain unresolved.

The rest of the full baseline remains implementation/acceptance work, including richer persistent scan indexes, resumable mutation/provider jobs, quality-category tuning, broader video profiles and durable video batches, additional image format profiles, contact mutations/restoration, provider mutations, multipart export, richer review/organization and background operation. No feature has been cut merely to fit this first build. Email/vault remain candidates; treemap stays deferred.

## Resumable media comparisons

Completed full-resource measurements are checkpointed in native SQLite on both phones, excluded from backups and kept separate from review decisions/history. A new foreground comparison always refreshes the accessible inventory, prunes deleted/revoked/changed entries, and reuses only a matching asset identity and provider revision. A checkpoint is durable before progress advances. Cancellation or app termination retains completed resources; partial resources are remeasured. Clearing scan progress removes derived measurements only.

Android 11+ revisions include MediaStore's volume database version and generation, following the provider contract. Older Android versions and PhotoKit assets without a modification date are still compared but do not retain reusable measurements. A changed provider epoch invalidates earlier measurements. Cloud originals remain opt-in on iOS. This delivers resumable foreground exact comparisons, not background scanning, a mutation journal or all large-library performance acceptance.

## Date review sessions

Photos and videos can be filtered by an exact day, a month including its year, a year, or the same month/day in earlier years (On This Day). The chosen reference is explicit and remains fixed until edited. Dates come from the library, interpreted as Gregorian calendar dates in the device’s current time zone. Unknown dates appear only with Any date; no scan date is invented. February 29 is never shifted to February 28 or March 1.

Both phones use native date pickers and explicit Apply/Cancel/Clear controls. Changing a filter preserves selections and durable keep/later/removal decisions. Android retains both committed filters and open filter drafts across configuration changes. These are media filters; the folder filters below use modification dates. Persistent review sessions and background reminders remain separate baseline work.

## Folder filtering

The scanned-folder list on both phones supports filename extension, decimal size thresholds (1/10/100 MB and 1 GB), explicit unknown-size selection, modification-date scopes and exact-duplicate filtering alongside filename search. Extensions are derived from names, not content inspection. Zero-byte files are known sizes; missing or negative provider sizes are unknown. Missing modification dates match only Any date.

Filters edit a draft until Apply; Cancel leaves the current result unchanged. The list shows matched/total counts and active constraints, with clear controls. Clear filters preserves the search query. Android retains the filters, query and an open filter draft across configuration changes and keeps applied filters across tab changes. Filtering never modifies the scan, original files or review decisions. Provider mutations, file previews, export and durable provider jobs remain separate baseline work.

## Data and platform

Native SwiftUI, iOS/iPadOS 18+. Foundation-only CleanerCore owns decisions and preservation rules. CleanerPlatform uses Apple frameworks and pinned PhoneNumberKit. Review state writes atomically and unreadable state fails closed. Library resource sizes on older SDKs are measured from requested original resources; unknown is never zero. Cloud downloads are off by default. No private PhotoKit size API.

User content stays in Apple Photos/Contacts/Files unless a copy or export is explicitly requested. App-private review metadata is local. Existing decisions/history and existing contact exports remain accessible after trial expiry; new scans and cleanup actions require trial/unlock. The latest contact export the person made is kept on the phone only: in the app container with complete file protection and excluded from backups on iOS, and in no-backup app storage on Android. Contacts discloses it, shares it again after expiry or relaunch, and deletes it on request. Merge review counts a backup as verified only when that read-back archive holds the exact record versions being compared. Debug fixtures use isolated state and never establish production purchase evidence.

## Bounded native video copies — 2026-11-18

Both phones now offer Trim or compress from a video detail. Android uses Material RangeSlider, labelled decimal fields, an explicit prepared preview, native playback and a fixed Save copy/Retry action; iOS uses native SwiftUI fields, AVPlayer and toolbar actions. A changed range requires a new preview. Drafts survive Android recreation; preparation can be cancelled. Outputs remain separate copies and never authorize removing the original.

The initial profile accepts identified 8-bit BT.709 SDR H.264, one video track and optional mono/stereo AAC, constant 1–30 fps, up to 1080p, 10 minutes and 512 MiB. Clips must be at least 0.5 second. Output is H.264/AAC, no larger than 1280 × 720 or its portrait counterpart, without enlargement. Media3 Transformer and AVFoundation own encoding. Unsupported codecs, HDR/unknown color, auxiliary/multipart resources and unsupported timing/transforms are refused. This is a bounded first profile, not full video-format support.

Actual output dimensions, duration, frame rate, audio format and sampled decoded frames are checked. Duration tolerance is 100 ms. Native fixture tests additionally compare selected upright source/output frames and decode the entire audio stream to PCM. Android checks capture date and latitude/longitude; descriptive container tags and altitude are explicitly excluded. Apple copies/checks supported string metadata, excluding changed encoder provenance. Library date/favorite/location/albums or folder are retained where the platform/provider supports them. Output can be larger; the review warns rather than promising savings.

The original resource read is bounded and cancellable. Temporary-capacity checks precede encoding; errors/cancellation clean owned candidates. Saving reads the created provider resource back before recording completion. If recording fails after creation, the in-memory receipt retries the same output, preserving the original and its review decision. Source/output guards prevent repeated lossy processing. Drafts and unfinished receipts remain session-only; provider creation and the ledger are not crash-atomic. Broader HDR/HEVC/auxiliary profiles, Live-to-still, durable/background video batches, resource-pressure and physical-provider acceptance remain open baseline work.

## Review safety, keepers and filters — 2026-11-22

Deletion checks each queued item, not just the batch. The core reports every item as ready, changed since it was queued, a favorite, not marked or no longer available. Only ready items are offered to Photos or the Android trash; the batch guard still refuses any other item. Pending removal opens against the current library, marks changed and favorite items, and offers review, confirming again at the current revision, or removing them from the queue. A favorite change refreshes the item from the provider on both phones, so later copies and decisions bind to its new revision.

Selections are scoped to the library on screen: Photos never acts on a selected video, and Select visible or Clear selection leaves the other library's selection alone. Compare groups label the core's suggested keeper with the rule that chose it: kept, favorite, most pixels, earliest capture or name order. The label is a suggestion; similar groups are never preselected. Similar shots are connected groups in the core: nearby pairs join closest first, up to 20 photos, so a burst is one group rather than overlapping pairs.

Photos and videos filter by size (the folder size thresholds), shorter-side dimensions and, for videos, length, plus a Large collection (photos of at least 10 MB, videos of at least 100 MB). Unknown size, dimensions or length match only their Unknown choice and are never treated as zero. Still compression checks free space for the candidate and its read-back before encoding.

Android has folder albums: an album is `Pictures/<name>/`. Android 11+ moves the selected items there after the system's write confirmation; Android 10+ can instead copy them there, reading each copy back byte for byte and leaving the originals in place. Android 8–9 cannot create albums. A moved item has a new revision, so earlier removal decisions need review again. Android previews apply EXIF orientation.
